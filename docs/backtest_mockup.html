Read before trusting the numbers

  • Return basis differs. The portfolio is total return on adjusted prices, with cash dividends reinvested on the ex-date. VNINDEX, VN30 and VN100 are price indexes, so the portfolio leads them by roughly the dividend yield before any skill.
  • Today's book, held backwards. Group map, ratings, deleted and invalidated names are as of the anchor, 2026-09-11, and apply to every past date. The universe is today's 100 names, so survivorship and look-ahead bias flatter every level.
  • Rebalances follow the mandate. A scheduled rebalance re-derives group budgets from that session's free float × official close and re-solves the constraints. A drift rebalance restores the last target without re-deriving it.
  • Cash until the first fill. The benchmark counts from the start close; the portfolio buys at the close after the fill lag.
  • Short window. Annualised figures stretch fewer than 252 sessions to a year; read total return and excess first.
