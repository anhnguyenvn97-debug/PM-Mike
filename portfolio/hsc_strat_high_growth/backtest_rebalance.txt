backtest_rebalance.json - data dictionary
hand-maintained; edit this alongside the .json when a key changes

WHAT IT IS
  The rebalancing discipline: how often target weights are recalculated, what
  drift is tolerated between recalculations, and what a trade costs. It is a
  property of the strategy, not of a backtest run -- the variant list and the
  output folder stay CLI flags. The date range lives HERE (start/end), because
  a strategy backtested over different windows is a different evidence base.

  This copy belongs to portfolio/hsc_strat_high_growth/ and OVERRIDES the
  default in portfolio/baseline/. Edits to the baseline copy do NOT propagate
  here. A portfolio with no copy of its own inherits baseline's. It sits above
  input/, so build_portfolio.py --fork can never clobber it -- same reason
  sector_cap.json and tactical_group.json live where they do.

KEYS

  rebalance            yes | no
                       "no" means buy-and-hold from inception and NOTHING else
                       in this file is read. A missing file is the same as
                       "no", matching how sector_cap.json already behaves.

  calendar             monthly | quarterly
                       Scheduled recalculation frequency.

  on                   first_session
                       Which session of the period the rebalance lands on.
                       Resolved against sessions actually present in
                       local_history.db, so short months need no special case.

  start                dd-mm-yyyy | default
  end                  dd-mm-yyyy | default
                       Backtest window, inclusive, DAY-FIRST format. "default"
                       resolves to the first / last session in
                       local_history.db. A dated start snaps FORWARD to the
                       first session on or after it; a dated end snaps BACK to
                       the last session on or before it, so weekends and
                       holidays need no special care.

  anchor_on_rebalance  yes | no
                       yes: baseline budgets b_g re-derived from that session's
                            float cap. Ratings and multipliers are an overlay on
                            a moving base.
                       no:  target computed once at inception and frozen. Kept
                            as a DIAGNOSTIC only -- it isolates how much of a
                            book's return comes from the tilt versus from
                            tracking a drifting float-cap base. Not a production
                            setting: by 2026-07-01 a frozen target sits 25.1% of
                            NAV away from the live float-cap target (20.4% at
                            group level), can never hold the five names that
                            listed mid-period (2.3% of the book), and ignores
                            free-float changes in 85 of 102 tickers. It also
                            makes drift_threshold meaningless, since the band
                            would then maintain a stale target precisely.
                       Neither setting moves the MEMBER LIST. local_history.db
                       holds one frozen universe with no membership history, so
                       every run is on current constituents held backwards.
                       That survivorship bias is stamped into built_from.txt.

  drift_band           yes | no
                       Off-schedule trigger between calendar dates. "no" means
                       calendar only and drift_grain/drift_threshold are unread.

  drift_grain          group | name
                       Level the drift is summed over. group uses the 21 sector
                       budgets, name uses the 102 holdings. Name-level is always
                       the larger of the two -- within-group dispersion cancels
                       when summed to the group (median ratio 1.40, max 2.79 on
                       2025-01-02..2026-08-18).
                       group is correct here: within-group weights are float-cap
                       arithmetic, not decisions. Stock selection is expressed by
                       deleting names; after that the survivors' relative sizes
                       are an artifact, and a name-grain band would trade to
                       restore a relationship nobody chose. Every other rule in
                       the repo -- ratings, multipliers, sector cap, tactical
                       overlay -- operates at the group grain.

  drift_threshold      fraction, not percent (0.05 = 5%)
                       Fires when 0.5 * sum_g |w_g - t_g| exceeds it.
                       The metric is one-way turnover-to-restore, so the
                       threshold is denominated in what you pay rather than in
                       an abstract distance. At group grain it UNDER-reads the
                       actual trade: across sessions where group drift sat near
                       5%, real name-level turnover to restore was a median
                       6.13% (range 4.89-7.97%). rebalances.csv logs both -- the
                       group drift that triggered the event, and the name-level
                       turnover actually transacted.
                       There is no drift_metric key: turnover-to-restore has no
                       alternative worth switching to.

  lag_sessions         integer, sessions between decision and fill
                       1 = observe session t's close, compute targets, fill at
                       t+1. 0 assumes you can trade the print you just measured.

  execution_price      close_adj | open_adj
                       Fill price. This DOMINATES the cost model: median
                       |close_adj - open_adj| is 1.08% of close (p90 3.52%),
                       larger than any plausible brokerage assumption. Must be
                       identical across all variants in a sweep or the
                       comparison is meaningless.

  brokerage_bps        basis points per side. 10 = 0.10%.
                       NOTE 1 bp = 0.01%; 10 bps = 0.10%. The two are not the
                       same number.

  sell_tax_bps         basis points on sell proceeds. 10 = 0.1%.
                       Modelled separately from brokerage so summary.csv can
                       show turnover cost and tax drag as distinct lines.

  Annual cost = one-way turnover * (2 * brokerage_bps + sell_tax_bps) / 10000.

BEHAVIOUR NOT EXPOSED AS CONFIG
  - A band-triggered rebalance RESTORES the current target; it does not
    re-anchor. Re-anchoring is a calendar event only.
  - Restore is to full target, never trimmed to the band edge. Trim-to-edge
    leaves the book sitting on the threshold where any further drift re-fires,
    which is the design that needs a cooldown parameter. This one does not: at
    threshold 0.05 the tightest gap between band triggers is 32 sessions and the
    closest approach to a scheduled rebalance is 17.
  - Late listings enter at the next scheduled rebalance after their first
    session. Until then their group's existing names carry the weight; no cash
    is held aside and no off-cycle trade is forced.
  - A name with no price that session is held and corrected at the next
    tradeable session. BSR and TAL are each missing 8 interior sessions, MCH 5.
  - Float cap is always free_float * (market_cap / outstanding_shares), the
    same basis as build_baseline.py. On rows where that identity disagrees with
    close_raw -- load_history.py warns on roughly 1.5% of rows -- the derived
    value is used anyway, for exact consistency with the live builder, and the
    count is logged per rebalance date.

WHY THESE DEFAULTS
  Measured on the Default variant, 102 names, 403 sessions 2025-01-02 ->
  2026-08-18, quarterly re-anchor, T+1 fill.

  Aggregate group drift between quarterly rebalances, % of NAV:
      median 3.16   p75 4.37   p90 5.90   p99 8.13   max 8.72

  Drift does not spike and revert; it climbs early in the quarter and stays.
  Any threshold at or below ~3% therefore sits inside the normal operating
  range and turns the band into the schedule rather than an exception. 0.05
  sits above p75.

      threshold   scheduled   band   total   turnover/yr
      off                 6      0       6         10.5%
      0.02                6     22      28         69.5%
      0.03                6     13      19         60.3%
      0.04                6      5      11         39.0%
      0.05                6      3       9         30.4%

  At brokerage 10 bps/side plus 10 bps sell tax, cost = turnover * 30/10000:
      band on  (30.4% turnover)   9.1 bps/yr
      band off (10.5% turnover)   3.2 bps/yr
  The whole band decision is worth roughly 6 bps a year.
